What to pack for Umrah: a complete checklist

A practical packing list for Umrah from the UAE, organised by category, with what to carry in your hand luggage and what to leave at home.

Updated 29 October 2026Written by the nūrah teamReview: qualified scholar (to be confirmed before publication)
In this guide Before you pack Documents Ihram and clothing Fragrance-free care Prayer essentials Health and comfort Hand luggage What to leave at home Questions

Before you pack

Umrah requires very little, but a few items matter a great deal. Pack light: you will walk long distances, and most hotels near the Haram offer laundry services. If you plan to enter ihram on the flight, keep your ihram and toiletries in your hand luggage. Our guide to entering ihram on a flight explains why.

Tick items off as you pack. Your progress is saved in this browser, and you can print the list.

What to leave at home

  • Perfumes and scented products, including scented wet wipes and the scented items often found in airline amenity kits.
  • Valuables you do not need, and large amounts of cash.
  • More clothes than you need. Laundry services are widely available.
  • Heavy books. The nūrah preparation guide is also available digitally through the QR code in your order.

Questions

How many ihram sets should I take for Umrah?

We recommend two sets, so that you have a spare if one becomes wet or soiled during the journey.

Can I carry liquids in my hand luggage?

Airline security rules usually limit liquids in hand luggage to containers of 100 ml or less. Pack travel sizes of your fragrance-free toiletries if you plan to use them on the flight.

What if I forget something?

Essentials can be bought in Makkah and Madinah, although choice and prices vary. Ordering before you travel lets you check products, sizes and fragrance-free labels in advance.
