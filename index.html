Frequently asked questions

Answers are general guidance and are pending review by a qualified scholar. Follow official Nusuk guidance and ask your scholar about your own situation.

Do women need to wear white?
No. In the state of ihram, women wear ordinary modest clothing in any colour, provided it is loose and not sheer. Women do not wear a face veil (niqab) or gloves while in ihram. Our Umrah abayas are plain; decorated pieces are marked for after the journey. Women's essentials
When should I change into ihram if I am flying?
Ihram is entered before crossing the miqat. On a flight the aircraft may cross the miqat well before landing, so many pilgrims change before boarding and make their intention before the miqat. Read Entering ihram on a flight, and confirm the timing with your scholar and your airline.
Which fabric is best for summer?
Air, our lightest fabric, has an open waffle weave suited to warm climates and long days on foot. Compare all four fabrics
Can I use these toiletries while in ihram?
Scented products are not permitted in the state of ihram. Our care sets are fragrance-free and unflavoured. For products bought elsewhere, read every label and ask your scholar if you are unsure.
Will it arrive before I travel?
Orders placed before 2pm are delivered the next day across the UAE. Add your travel date above, or in your bag, and we will check the arrival date against your departure. Delivery and returns
