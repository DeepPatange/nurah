Entering ihram on a flight: miqat guidance

If you fly to Jeddah for Umrah, your flight will usually pass a miqat before it lands. How to prepare at home, at the airport and on board, so you are ready before you reach it.

Updated 29 October 2026Written by the nūrah teamReview: qualified scholar (to be confirmed before publication)

Please note: this guide is general preparation information based on official Nusuk guidance. It is not a religious ruling. Ask a qualified scholar about your own itinerary.

What a miqat is

The miqat are boundaries around Makkah. A pilgrim travelling to perform Umrah or Hajj enters the state of ihram before passing the miqat on their route.

Entering ihram and wearing the ihram are not the same thing. Men wear two unstitched cloths, the izar and the rida, but wearing the cloth alone does not begin ihram. The state of ihram begins with the intention, made at or before the miqat.

Why it matters when flying

When you travel by air on a route that crosses a miqat, you must enter the state of ihram before the aircraft passes it. For flights from the UAE to Jeddah, this happens in the air, before landing. Waiting until you arrive at Jeddah airport is generally too late for Umrah.

The exact point depends on your route, so it helps to prepare early and know what to do on board.

Before you leave home

  • Pack your ihram, belt, sandals and fragrance-free toiletries in your hand luggage. Our Umrah packing checklist lists everything.
  • Many pilgrims complete personal grooming and bathe at home before departure.
  • Download the Nusuk app and check that your permit is in order, where required.
  • Ask your travel operator which route your flight takes, and ask your scholar when to make the intention.

At the airport

Many men change into the ihram garments at home or at the departure airport, where prayer rooms and changing areas are available. Changing early means you only need to make the intention on board, rather than change in a small aircraft washroom.

Keep your documents, phone and cards in an ihram belt, and keep your regular clothes in your hand luggage for after the rites.

On the flight

  • Many airlines announce when the aircraft is approaching the miqat. If you are unsure, ask the cabin crew in advance.
  • Make the intention before the aircraft passes the miqat, and begin the talbiyah.
  • Decline scented items such as perfumed wet wipes or amenity kits, as fragrance restrictions apply in the state of ihram.
  • Stay hydrated, and rest if you can. You may begin the rites soon after you arrive in Makkah.
Prepare for the flight

For women

Women do not wear a special white garment. They enter ihram in their usual modest clothing, and they also make the intention before the miqat. Specific rules apply to women in the state of ihram; ask your scholar, and see our women's range for breathable, modest clothing suitable for the journey.

Travelling to Madinah first

If you fly to Madinah and visit before travelling on to Makkah, you usually do not need to enter ihram on your flight. Instead, you enter ihram when you set off for Makkah, at the miqat on that route, known as Dhul Hulaifah or Abyar Ali.

Questions

Can I wear my ihram garments from the UAE?

Yes. Many men put on the ihram garments at home or at the departure airport and make the intention later, before the aircraft reaches the miqat.

When exactly should I make the intention?

Before passing the miqat on your route. Many airlines announce when the aircraft is approaching it. Ask a qualified scholar about the timing for your itinerary.

What if I fly to Madinah first?

If you visit Madinah before Makkah, you usually enter ihram when you travel on to Makkah, at the miqat on that route, rather than on your flight.

What if I pass the miqat without entering ihram?

Guidance depends on the circumstances. Please ask a qualified scholar.
