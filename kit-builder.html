    Questions

    Kit FAQs

    Can I change items after ordering?

    Yes, until your order is packed. Contact us on WhatsApp with your order number and we will update it. Final policy to be confirmed.

    Can I send the kit to someone else?

    Yes. Enter their address at checkout and select gift wrap to add a note and hide prices on the packing slip.

    Do you offer kits for groups?

    Yes. Umrah agencies, group leaders and community organisations can request co-branded kits and group pricing. See Groups and agencies.

    What if it does not arrive before I travel?

    The expected arrival date is shown before you pay and checked against your travel date. If an order arrives after you have travelled, you may return it unopened for a refund. Final policy to be confirmed.
