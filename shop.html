Women

Women's clothing and essentials

Women do not need a white two-piece ihram. Modest, breathable abayas, a two-piece prayer set and hijabs, with an unscented care set.

In the state of ihram, women wear ordinary modest clothing in any colour, loose and not sheer, and do not wear a face veil (niqab) or gloves. Our Umrah pieces are plain; decorated pieces are marked for after the journey.

Accessories

Good to know before you travel
Umbrella

Men in ihram should hold the umbrella above them, never rest anything on the head. Scholars differ on shade overhead; ask yours if unsure.

Care products

Fragrance is not permitted in the state of ihram. Our care products are fragrance-free and unflavoured; read every label on items bought elsewhere.
