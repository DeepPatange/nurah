Details

Product information

Customer reviews are displayed from verified purchases only and will appear after launch.
Specifications

Composition, dimensions and weight will be published from verified supplier samples. Lengths: Regular (up to 180 cm) and Long (180 cm and above).

Size guide

Choose Regular if you are up to 180 cm tall and Long if you are taller. The upper cloth is the same size in both options.

Fabric and care

Machine wash at 30°C with a fragrance-free detergent. Line dry. Do not tumble dry.

Suitability for ihram

Guidance on wearing ihram is provided in the printed guide, which is reviewed by a qualified scholar. For specific questions, please consult your scholar.

Delivery and returns

Next-day delivery across the UAE for orders placed before 2pm. Unworn items may be returned within 14 days.
