Why we started

Our story

Buying for Umrah meant several shops, unfamiliar materials and guesswork. We set out to make it simpler: useful products, accurate information and delivery on time.

What we make

Materials and quality

Every product lists composition, size, weight and care, taken from tested samples.

How we check what we say

Religious content review

Ritual guidance, duas and statements about suitability for ihram are checked by a qualified scholar before publication and reviewed every year.

  • Sources: official Nusuk and Ministry of Hajj and Umrah guidance
  • Reviewer name and date shown on every guide
  • Corrections published openly
